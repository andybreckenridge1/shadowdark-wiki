You were sold into bondage to the Dark Lord, known as Master Archon, who rules from an island fortress in the Aegean Sea. His will is enforced by his right hand, Master Karos, a giant of a man with cracked, ash-black skin who carries a greatsword whose pommel glows like hot iron.

Three weeks ago, Karos led an expedition of one hundred slaves and one hundred mercenaries of the XY Company from Lydos to the Skull Coast in the eastern Mediterranean. Once part of the mighty Assyrian Empire, which fell more than a century ago, this land is now overrun by orcs, gnolls, ogres, and worse.

The expedition established a fortified hilltop encampment near the mouth of Demre Canyon, overlooking the lagoon where the ships lay at anchor. Each morning at dawn, you marched up the canyon to excavate shafts into ancient landslide deposits using picks and hammers. What you were searching for, no one would tell you. Questions earned the whip. Each evening, you returned to the encampment.

On the final day of your captivity, your crew of four broke through the rubble into a buried chamber, uncovering massive double doors decorated with a sunburst. A messenger was immediately dispatched to inform Karos of the discovery.

Two hours later, an alarm horn sounded from a nearby guard outpost. The expedition was under attack. In the ensuing confusion, your crew was left unguarded before the forbidden doors.
