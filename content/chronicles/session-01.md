As the alarm horn reverberated down the shaft, the party retreated into the stone room containing the great double doors bearing the sunburst icon—the very chamber they had been told not to enter.

In an adjoining room, the party discovered lockers and a visitor logbook. The most recent entries dated back 128 years, to the Great Cataclysm, according to Kayne the dwarf scholar. Two lockers appeared to contain weapons that had not been checked out, one belonging to Lucius Telius Felix and the other to Decimus Varenus. Bert and Ernie boldly investigated, only to be rushed by two giant centipedes emerging from the rubble. Ernie dispatched both, suffering a bite in the process. A shortsword and dagger appeared to have been removed from Varenus’ locker, leaving behind a bow, while Felix’s locker contained everything listed in the logbook.

Short on torches and other supplies, the party backtracked. Hop turned invisible and snuck outside to discover a scene of devastation. Among the carnage lay the dead lieutenant who had overseen their crew and a wounded orc. Kayne dropped the orc with a magic missile. Whatever the attackers had come for, they had shown little interest in the expedition’s supplies. The party looted the brigade’s tent for backpacks, torches, rations, and rope, leaving behind the water barrels, spears, and clubs.

Newly outfitted, the party returned underground and ventured through the great sunburst doors. Beyond stood fine marble statues of three of the Muses—Erato, Clio, and Calliope—and a great crystal tetrahedron suspended from the high ceiling. Bert lit incense, prayed to Calliope, and collected robes for the party before they ventured deeper into the complex.

A long, curving hallway led to a great library filled with rows of books and scrolls. Unlike the pristine entrance chamber, the library smelled of mildew, its shelves had been disturbed, and numerous tracks crisscrossed the floor.

Investigating a desk and cedar box on the far side of the room, the party discovered a cataloging system. Upon learning that the library contained books on magic and the occult, Kayne hastily led everyone to that section. So engrossed was the dwarf in the books that he alone failed to notice three Rodents of Unusual Size skittering from the rubble of the partially collapsed ceiling.

Will Kayne bother to tear himself away from the books? Will Hop think of something that rhymes with rat? Will Ernie sit this one out? Stay tuned.
