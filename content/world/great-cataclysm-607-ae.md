A series of natural disasters that shattered the ancient world 128 years ago. The volcanic island of Santorin, home to one of the three great dwarven kingdoms, erupted catastrophically and triggered a tsunami that wrecked coastal settlements throughout the Aegean. Massive earthquakes devastated Anatolia and the Balkans. Comets streaked across the heavens, and tremendous airbursts rained fire upon the earth, igniting widespread wildfires. The cause of these disasters remains disputed. The Great War followed the Cataclysm.
