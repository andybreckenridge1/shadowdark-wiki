Warrior-priestess of Athena, martyr, protector, healer, and defender of the good. At Troy she sacrificed herself to open a passage to the Outer Plane and restore Hercules. Her shield maidens buried her on the Isle of Allea. The Allean Order, founded in 655 AE, promotes peace, justice, and protection of the weak. Its lawful priestess-warriors advise rulers and mediate disputes; the Temple of Alleah is in Athens.
