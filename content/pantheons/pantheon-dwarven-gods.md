### Moradin

The Soul Forger, creator of the dwarves and patron of smithing, craftsmanship, and creation.

### Berronar Truesilver

Goddess of family, marriage, truth, loyalty, and the preservation of dwarven communities.

### Clangeddin Silverbeard

God of battle, courage, martial discipline, and defense of the dwarven people.

### Dumathoin

Guardian of the secrets beneath the mountains, mining, mineral wealth, and hidden knowledge.

### Vergadain

God of commerce, wealth, cunning, negotiation, and luck; especially important among expatriate dwarven merchants.

### Abbathor

God of greed and avarice, embodying the dangerous desire to accumulate treasure.
