Priestesses led the revolt against Assyrian rule in 630 AE. The restored temples have since become centers of political authority, magical learning, and religious life.

Individual temples frequently disagree over what constitutes balance, giving rise to political rivalries.

Isis, the Mother and Restorer — Goddess of magic, healing, fertility, and motherhood. Her priestesses are influential in the Nile city-states; she embodies creation, compassion, and renewal.

Osiris, Lord of the Dead — God of death, resurrection, and the afterlife. His priests oversee funerary rites and the passage of souls into the Outer Plane; death is an essential part of the cycle of existence.

Set, Lord of Chaos — God of storms, deserts, violence, and upheaval. Feared but not universally regarded as evil, he represents destructive forces that challenge order and make renewal possible.

Other manifestations survive in lesser cults.
