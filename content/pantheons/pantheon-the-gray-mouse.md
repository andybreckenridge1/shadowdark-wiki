Halfling thief and hero slain at Troy. Patron of cunning, luck, travel, clever survival, thieves, merchants, scouts, messengers, gamblers, and the poor. Followers leave coins, food, and small valuables at shrines. His divinity is debated; most halflings regard him as the mortal embodiment of Brandobaris.

## Signature priest spells

### Lucky Break — Tier 1 • Close • Instant

Target rerolls a d20 and must use the new result. In combat the priest must have a turn remaining and immediately forfeits their action turn. Once per hour.

### Traveler’s Tongue — Tier 1 • Self • 10 rounds

Understand and speak any living mortal language heard, with an obvious foreign accent. Does not permit reading or writing. Also available to priests of Vergadain.
