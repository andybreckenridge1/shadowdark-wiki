Goddess of wisdom, strategy, learning, and civilization; patron of scholars, soldiers, rulers, judges, and craftsmen. Tradition holds that she acted through her priestess Alleah when unable to intervene openly during the Great War. Athens is her patron city.

## Signature priest spells

### Aegis of Athena — Tier 1 • Near • Focus

One ally gains +2 AC while you maintain focus. Cannot target yourself.

### Battle Wisdom — Tier 1 • Close • 5 rounds

Group initiative (1d6): party gains +1 to initiative. Individual initiative (1d20): priest and all allies gain +2 to initiative.
