Son of Zeus, barbarian warrior and defender of humankind. Patron of strength, courage, endurance, heroic deeds, warriors, laborers, athletes, and those facing great trials. Slain at Troy in 635 AE, he returned through Alleah’s sacrifice and carried Lucius into the Olympian realm. His first great temple was built at Olympia (renamed Herculeam/Herculeum) in 650 AE.

## Signature priest spells

### Might of Hercules — Tier 1 • Close • 3 rounds

One character you touch gains +2 STR.

### Lion’s Hide — Tier 1 • Self • 5 rounds

The hide of the Nemean Lion reduces weapon damage by 1 at levels 1–3, 2 at levels 4–6, and 3 at levels 7–10.
