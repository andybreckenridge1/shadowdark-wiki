### Yondalla

Mother and protector of the halfling people, associated with fertility, prosperity, and community.

### Arvoreen

God of vigilance, defense, and protection of halfling settlements.

### Brandobaris

Trickster god of stealth, thievery, daring adventures, and narrow escapes. Most halflings regard the Gray Mouse as his mortal embodiment.

### Cyrrollalee

Goddess of friendship, hospitality, trust, and the sanctity of the home.

### Sheela Peryroyl

Goddess of agriculture, nature, weather, and rural life.

### Urogalan

God of the earth and the dead, associated with burial and the passage from life to death.
