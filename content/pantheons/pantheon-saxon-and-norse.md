Norse and Saxon peoples honor the ancient Germanic gods. Woden (Odin) is lord of wisdom, magic, kingship, war, and the dead. Thunor (Thor) is the thunderer and protector of humankind. Frigg is the goddess of motherhood, marriage, foresight, and the household, while Freyja presides over love, fertility, sorcery, and the fallen in battle. Freyr is associated with prosperity, peace, and bountiful harvests. Sacred groves, shrines, and temples spread with Saxon and Norse settlements.
