Master Archon and Karos committed considerable resources to excavating the ancient landslides of Demre Canyon – why? The underground complex containing statues of the Muses and a great library raises numerous questions. The visitor logbook suggests that the complex was occupied until the time of the Great Cataclysm, 128 years ago. Although parts of the complex appear remarkably well preserved, others show evidence of collapse, disturbance, and more recent activity.

Is this complex what Karos was searching for, or was its discovery incidental to a greater objective?

## Unresolved questions

- What was the original purpose of the underground complex?

- Who occupied it before the Great Cataclysm, and what became of them?

- What is Karos searching for, and why is it important to him?

- Was the attack on the expedition connected to its excavations, or was it a coincidence?

The answers may reveal something about the ambitions of Karos and Master Archon, as well as the history of the world before the Great Cataclysm.
